About this game

Explore infinite worlds and build everything from the simplest of homes to the grandest of castles. Play in creative mode with unlimited resources or mine deep into the world in survival mode, crafting weapons and armor to fend off dangerous mobs.

Main Features:

1️⃣ Survive and build your own world
2️⃣ Cross-platform play for up to eight players across Windows, PlayStation, Nintendo, Xbox, and mobile devices
3️⃣ Discover massive community-run multiplayer servers
4️⃣ Expand your game with the Marketplace
5️⃣ Slash commands to tweak how the game plays
6️⃣ Ray tracing for Windows brings a different graphical experience
